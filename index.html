BEFORE YOU PRESS PLAY.

Can I log workouts and play for free?

Yes. Recording your workouts and playing the game are entirely free.

How do workouts make my fighter stronger?

Each saved workout earns one upgrade point, up to three points per day. Spend them in Stats on Health, Damage, Magic, Speed / Recovery, or Special. Every workout counts toward your overall level, even after the daily point allowance. Leveling up also adds one automatic upgrade to all five categories. The point allowance does not limit how many workouts you can log.

Does my game save automatically?

Yes. Your completed levels save automatically on your device. When you close the app and return to Play, those levels stay cleared. There is one default save, with no save-slot selection.

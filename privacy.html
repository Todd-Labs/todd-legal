Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Todd Labs handles information through the Todd iOS app and the Todd Labs public website. Todd Labs is responsible for the practices described here.

Privacy at a Glance

Todd's workout tracking and solo game do not require an account, login, or email address. Optional online multiplayer requires Apple Game Center sign-in. Workout records, templates, goals, profile settings, trends, and solo game progress are stored on your device by default.

The current version of Todd does not use an artificial intelligence provider, does not offer voice entry, does not record your microphone, and does not send audio to Deepgram or any other transcription provider. Todd does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information leaves your device for the limited purposes described below: optional Game Center multiplayer, subscription management, an issue report you choose to submit, a support email you choose to send, or ordinary delivery of the public website.

Information Stored on Your Device

You may enter a display name, weekly training target, workout dates and notes, exercises, sets, repetitions, weight, rest, time, distance, steps, templates, and fitness goals. Todd also stores preferences, notification choices, completed-goal history, game settings, and story save progress.

Todd uses this local information to display your history, calculate goals and trends, schedule local reminders, and calculate game stats and progress. Core fitness records are stored with Apple's SwiftData technology. Preferences and game saves use on-device system storage. Entering this information does not by itself send it to Todd Labs.

Todd does not currently provide an account-based cloud sync or backup service. Data therefore may not move automatically to another device.

Issue Reports and Support

Issue reports are optional. If you submit one in the app, Todd sends the categories you select, your written description, app version and build number, a random installation identifier, request timing and basic technical metadata, and—when you report a particular workout or goal—a copy of that record. The report form tells you this before submission.

Reports are sent to Todd Labs through Supabase so they can be investigated and the app can be improved. Supabase and network providers may process your Internet Protocol address and connection information while transmitting the report. Issue reports are not sent to an AI model.

If you contact support by email, Todd Labs receives your email address and anything else you include in the message or attachments.

Optional Game Center Multiplayer

If you choose multiplayer, Apple Game Center handles authentication, invitations and player connections under Apple's privacy practices. Todd exchanges Game Center player identifiers and display names, equipped outfit and attack choices, readiness, character combat values, controls and match state with participants in your invited match. One participant's device hosts the match.

Todd does not send workout records, workout categories, profile details, email addresses or backpack contents to the other players. Character combat values can reflect your upgrades, but the underlying workout history is not shared. Todd's multiplayer implementation keeps remote match data in memory for the session and does not upload it to a Todd Labs game server. Apple may process service and connection information to provide Game Center.

Multiplayer has no built-in text or voice chat and does not automatically match you with strangers. You can leave a match at any time. Leaving or losing a participant connection ends this initial version's match. Multiplayer does not change solo progress or award or spend game coins. Single-player features remain available without Game Center sign-in.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Todd uses RevenueCat to load subscription offerings, validate receipts, restore purchases, prevent purchase fraud, understand subscription performance, and determine whether premium access is active.

RevenueCat may receive an anonymous App User ID generated by its software, app and device technical information, purchase receipts, purchase history, subscription status, and related timestamps. Todd does not give RevenueCat your name, workout history, goals, or game progress.

Local Notifications

Todd may ask for permission to schedule local notifications for weekly training reminders, goal and streak milestones, and an annual subscription renewal reminder. These are scheduled through iOS from information on your device; Todd Labs does not operate a push-notification server for them. You can change notification permission at any time in iPhone Settings.

Website Information

The Todd website is a static site hosted through GitHub Pages. Todd Labs does not place advertising cookies or run its own visitor analytics on the site. GitHub may process standard web request information, such as an Internet Protocol address, browser or device information, requested pages, and timestamps, to host and secure the site under GitHub's privacy practices. Selecting an email link opens your email provider; the site does not contain a support submission form.

How Information Is Used

Information sent off device is used to provide optional Game Center multiplayer, provide and verify subscriptions, respond to support, investigate user-reported problems, maintain security, prevent abuse or fraud, comply with law, and improve the reliability of Todd. Todd Labs does not use workout or goal information for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

Service Providers and Disclosures

Todd currently relies on these service providers for the limited purposes described above:

• Apple (apple.com/legal/privacy) — App Store distribution, in-app purchases, subscription management, optional Game Center multiplayer, and iOS platform services.

• RevenueCat (revenuecat.com/privacy) — subscription offering delivery, receipt validation, entitlement status, restoration, fraud prevention, and subscription analytics.

• Supabase (supabase.com/privacy) — secure receipt and storage of issue reports submitted from the app.

• GitHub Pages (docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) — hosting and security for the public website.

Todd Labs may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or complete a business transfer subject to appropriate notice and safeguards. Todd Labs does not authorize these providers to use Todd information for their own advertising.

Retention

Local information remains on your device until you delete it using an available in-app control or remove the app. An on-device support identifier may remain in the iOS Keychain after the app is removed. Issue reports are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate and resolve reports, maintain security, meet legal obligations, and document the resolution. Apple, RevenueCat, Supabase, GitHub, and email providers retain information under their own policies and Todd's service settings.

Deletion and Privacy Rights

You can delete individual workouts, goals, templates, and game saves where Todd provides a delete or reset control. Removing Todd deletes its ordinary local app storage, subject to iOS backups and Keychain behavior controlled by Apple. To request deletion of an issue report or other information held by Todd Labs, email support@toddlabs.info and include the approximate submission date, report type, and any text needed to locate it. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information. Todd Labs will respond as required by applicable law.

Security and International Processing

Todd Labs uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information sent to its service providers, including encrypted network connections. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate, subject to their legal and contractual safeguards.

Children

Todd is not directed to children under 13, and Todd Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted an issue report or support email can contact support@toddlabs.info to request deletion. Users who are not old enough to agree to these practices under local law should use Todd only with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes to This Policy

Todd Labs may update this Privacy Policy when the app, website, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will show a new effective date. If a future version adds a materially different data use, such as AI or voice processing, Todd Labs will update the policy and applicable App Store disclosures before that feature is made available.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests, and data-deletion requests can be sent to Todd Labs at support@toddlabs.info.

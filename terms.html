What Swole Streets Provides

Swole Streets combines manual workout logging, reusable templates, goals, calendar history, trends, local notifications, and widgets with a retro solo action game. Saved workouts contribute to game levels and upgrades. The current app does not provide AI coaching, voice transcription, or Apple Health integration. Multiplayer is not currently available from the app menu.

Game maps or catalog entries marked as future content do not promise an availability date. Features may evolve as the app is updated.

Free Access

Manual workout logging, workout editing, goal creation, saved history, Calendar, Trends, Profile, and the solo game are free. No subscription is required. There is no daily or lifetime creation cap on manual workouts or goals.

The game awards up to three spendable workout upgrade points per day. This is a game reward rule, not a limit on logging. Saved workouts with exercises still count toward overall game level after that allowance is reached. Level-ups add automatic upgrades to the five game categories.

Game Coins, Outfits, and Items

You can earn coins through play and spend them on the outfits, attack colors, and backpack items offered for coins in the Store. Outfits and attack colors are cosmetic. Backpack items have the effects described in the game and can be used up. Confirmed coin spending deducts from your local balance.

Coins, upgrade points, outfits, and other virtual items are for use only inside Swole Streets. They cannot be transferred, traded, redeemed for real money, or used for wagering. Some outfits and coin packs display real-money prices, but payment for those products is not enabled in the current build. A displayed price does not itself make a purchase or charge you. Trends, totals, goal percentages, and estimated one-repetition maximums are informational calculations and may be incomplete or inaccurate.
